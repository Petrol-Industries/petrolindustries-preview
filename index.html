Our DNA

Motor-centric thinking drives our direction.

At Petrol, we've made jeans wear and denim related products since 1989 — for a fair price and high quality. The heart of our heritage is in the car scene. We're equally inspired by the past, the present and the future: we love old cars, but we also love finding new ways to keep our freedom, hunt for adventure, and stay a little rebellious.

Every season we're on the hunt for new fits and old techniques to combine them with. Denim isn't just part of our range — it's part of our identity.
